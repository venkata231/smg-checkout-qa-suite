3. Exploratory Observations & Outcome

  • BUG-001: Search input loses focus when live search results update.
  • BUG-002: Purchased items remain in basket after successful checkout.
  • BUG-003: Basket quantity is capped at 5 despite higher available stock.
  • BUG-005: Checkout navigation continues to show "Pay" after successful order completion.

The findings from this exploratory session were used to inform a structured focused testing approach, where search/sorting, stock boundaries, promotional stacking rules, payment validation, duplicate order submission, order history, and CSV export were investigated systematically.
